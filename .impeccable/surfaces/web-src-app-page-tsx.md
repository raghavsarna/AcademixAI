# Surface: AcademixAI web (landing first; editions, papers, explain, library inherit)

Scope and mode: landing page `/` is Persuade. Edition pages and paper pages are Read; explain, library and auth are Operate. All share one world.
Audience: students, AI practitioners, curious non-experts. Job: see what changed in AI research today, then read any paper at their level.
Proof on hand: real daily editions (real arXiv papers, real counts of papers scanned), three-depth explanations of real papers. No users, testimonials or metrics exist; never invent them.
Constraints: daily data from the API; three depths (Plain, Student, Expert); chat needs an account.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The field of AI as a metro system. Research areas are lines, each day's papers are newly opened stations, and cross-listed papers are interchanges. Refuses the AI-SaaS hero with feature cards and the newsletter sign-up box.

OWN-WORLD: White signage ground, near-black ink, black signage bands with white type. Saturated line colors with fixed meanings (Language red, Vision blue, Learning green, Agents orange, Robotics purple, Other brown). Round line bullets, station ticks, white interchange capsules, 45 and 90 degree geometry. Archivo set like wayfinding signage, condensed for station names.

STORY: Visitors understand the field moved today and see exactly where; believe every entry is real and traceable to arXiv; ride today's changes, switch a paper to their depth, or explain their own PDF.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: a plain headline about today's change and one sentence on depths, primary action "Ride today's changes", secondary "Explain a paper". Full width below: today's live line diagram drawn from the real edition, stations labelled, one station open showing what changed. Primary action above the fold.

FORM: Vignelli-style metro diagram (my top-ranked grounded candidate, position 1). seed:389da9b357c2bcf0. Signature interaction: the line diagram draws its lines once, then focusing a station opens its station board; on phones it becomes a vertical strip map.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
